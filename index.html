Gold colors

Pick a color to load its ratio, then change the ratios of the alloy part (everything except gold) to tune the shade. Ratios must add up to 100. These are typical starting recipes, so melt a small test first. Nickel can cause skin allergies.

Solder recipes

Gold recipes are cadmium-free and use a little indium. Medium is the average of hard and easy. Silver recipes are for sterling silver work. Melt a small test and check the flow before using it on a piece.

Wire to strip

Rolling keeps the volume of metal the same. The wire whose cross-section equals the strip’s makes a strip of the same length, and a thicker wire makes a shorter piece. A good wire diameter lies between the strip thickness and width.

Estimates for stones, casting and wire are guides. Check critical jobs on a scale.
